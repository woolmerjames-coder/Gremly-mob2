# Turn triage: policy questions for the founder

- **Corrections and complaints about Gremly itself (14 questions).** No mode covers "you got that wrong" or "why can't you see my key dates". Labellers scattered across update, venting, emotional, app_help and quick_ask. Recommended default: label the request that is still unanswered (its mode and its depth). Use app_help for "why can't you see or remember X". Never use venting or emotional when the anger is at Gremly. Consider a dedicated repair template: own it in one line, then fix it.

- **Requests to make something: lists, plans, recipes (11 questions).** The labellers split between action_ready and research. The adjudication notes already give the rule, but the labelling guide does not. Recommended default: action_ready when they want something produced (packing list, list of things to do before a trip, weekly plan, recipe). Research when they want facts, options or ideas. Add this line to the guide.

- **Local places, travel and products: required or maybe search (9 questions).** Recommended default: required whenever the reply will name specific businesses, venues, tours, products or local rules. Maybe for the general feel of a destination, its climate, or broad gift ideas.

- **Health, supplement and nutrition search (8 questions).** The guide says health facts need a search, but many of these are follow ups or stable nutrition numbers. Recommended default: required when they ask about a named substance's effects, interactions, doses or evidence. Maybe for vague health questions, and for calories in a named restaurant dish. None for timing follow ups and standard nutrition values.

- **Vague openers such as "Help me plan" or "help me with this space" (5 questions).** Recommended default: brief. Ask one question that offers two or three choices drawn from the Space's own content, rather than guessing at a plan.

- **Lists versus the standard length cap (4 questions).** Checklists and comparisons across seasons or two cities strain standard's 150 word limit, so labellers reached for detailed. Recommended default: keep detailed for "break down", "full plan" or "in detail", and let standard run a little longer when the reply is a list.

- **A feeling plus a clear ask: how far the tie break reaches (3 questions).** Examples: "so much going on... can't figure out where to start" followed by a task list, or tiredness mentioned before asking for a suggestion. Recommended default: the feeling wins only when it is the main thing being said. When it is passing context for a clear ask, use the task mode and let the reply acknowledge the feeling in a line.

- **Reflective personal chats: emotional or exploratory (2 questions).** This matters because exploratory allows a save suggestion. Recommended default: emotional when they name a fear, worry or self doubt, exploratory otherwise. Also turn off save suggestions for exploratory chats about relationships or personal growth.
