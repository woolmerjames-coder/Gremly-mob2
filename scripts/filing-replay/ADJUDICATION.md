# Filing: for the adjudicator

The two labellers saw only LABEL_GUIDE.md. You also know what the app does
with a filing, so use that to settle the drops where they split.

## What happens with a filing

- The drop card shows where the drop went. The person can move it with one
  tap. Gremly never asks where a single drop belongs.
- A Chapter page lists what was filed into it, and Gremly writes the
  Chapter's words from what is there. A drop in the wrong Chapter shows up on
  a page about something else and can bend those words, so a wrong Chapter
  costs the most.
- A World is broad. A drop in a fair but not best World costs little.
- Filed nowhere, a drop is still in the person's lists; it just has no home
  on the Worlds screens. When the right place is unclear, nowhere is better
  than a wrong place.
- The bar James set: Chapter filings right 95 times in 100, World filings 85
  in 100.

## What to return

For every drop, the settled answer in the guide's shape, with "basis": "agreed"
when both labellers gave the same chapter and world, or "settled" with one
line on why when you chose. Where one World is right and another is fair, keep
the fair one in also_fair. Note any drop where you think the guide itself is
unclear in a separate list, "guide_questions".
